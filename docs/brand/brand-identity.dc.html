06 / Rationale
The trademark takes the oldest instrument in law — the balance — and removes its fulcrum, putting an AI core in its place. Everything the scale needs in order to be a scale now passes through a single luminous node: the beam is held level by inference, not by a pivot. Two teal paths climb from that core to a pair of source nodes above it, which is precisely what the product does — every answer is suspended from retrieved, citable law rather than asserted on its own authority. Drawn at hairline weight in semi-transparent white on obsidian, the geometry reads as instrumentation rather than heraldry: precise, unornamented, quiet. The glow is confined to the core and its retrieval paths, so the one thing that emits light in the entire mark is the moment where legislation becomes an answer.
